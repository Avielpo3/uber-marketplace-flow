One order, every hop

The real chain of order , from the Uber Direct webhooks to what Uber Eats accepted: which statuses and courier locations each service sent, and which ones stop on the way.

Timeline by hop

Each dot is one message seen at that hop. Hover or tap a dot for the details. A webhook goes straight down the lanes within about 50 ms; only PickedUp and Delivered become Uber Eats statuses, and locations wait for tracker's 30 s forwarding job, which sends the newest point only.

Courier path

The Uber Direct mock moves the courier on a straight line from the store to the customer (15 % to 75 % of the way). All 5 points reach tracker-service; Uber Eats receives the 3 that were newest when the forwarding job ran.

Step by step: what each hop sends

The two numbers are the delivery status and the courier status daas-uber puts on every status callback (codes below). tracker-service turns delivery status 3 into the order event Dispatched and 4 into Delivered; mp-uber sends those to Uber Eats as started and delivered. Everything else is stored but not sent to Uber Eats.
